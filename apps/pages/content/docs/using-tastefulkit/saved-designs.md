# Keep designs worth revisiting

Your Saved list is tied to your TastefulKit account. Sign in with the same account when you want to return to your references on another device.

## Save an example

1. Open a design from [Explore](/explore/).
2. Choose **Save design +**.
3. The button changes to **Saved ✓ · Remove**.
4. Open the library's [Saved tab](/explore/?saved=1) to see your saved references.

## Search your shortlist

Search and the **Element**, **Style**, and **Industry** filters work inside Saved too. They only return designs you have saved.

If your list looks empty, choose **Reset filters**. If you have not saved anything yet, return to **Explore**, open an example, and save it.

## Remove a design

Open the saved design and choose **Saved ✓ · Remove**. This removes it from your list, not from the catalogue. You can save it again later.

## Build a focused shortlist

Save references for specific decisions: one for navigation, another for typography, and another for page structure. Comparing a few clear examples is often more useful than collecting many without knowing what you like about them.

Saved designs remain subject to catalogue availability. An example that is no longer published will no longer appear in your list.
